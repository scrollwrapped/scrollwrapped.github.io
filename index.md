# Scroll Wrapped support

Scroll Wrapped turns the data you download from TikTok into a wrapped slideshow of your year: how long you scrolled, how many videos you watched, your busiest days, your scroll personality and more. Everything is worked out on your phone.

**Contact.** Questions, problems or ideas: scrollwrapped@gmail.com

**Getting your data from TikTok.** On TikTok, go to Profile and tap the menu at the top right. Tap Settings and privacy, then Account, then Download your data. Set the file format to JSON, keep everything selected and tap Request data. When it's ready, switch to the Download data tab, tap Download, save to Files, and open in Scroll Wrapped. The app walks you through each step with screenshots.

**How long does a TikTok Data Request take?** TikTok prepares the file itself. It's usually ready to download within a minute but can sometimes take longer.

**Is my data safe?** Your data never leaves your phone. The app has no account, no server, no analytics and no tracking. Read the [privacy policy](privacy).

**Deleting your data.** Tap Delete my data in the app, or delete the file from the app's folder in the Files app.

Scroll Wrapped is independent and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to TikTok or ByteDance.
